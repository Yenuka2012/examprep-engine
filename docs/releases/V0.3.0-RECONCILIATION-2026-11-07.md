## Mandatory rollback/data integrity rules

- v0.3.0 Dexie v3 upgrades underlying IndexedDB schema; the v0.2.1 app is not a safe downgrade for migrated profiles.
- Logical v2 encrypted backups require a v0.3.0-compatible reader; the prior reader rejects them.
- No IndexedDB deletion, cache clearing or destructive downgrade to fix a release issue. Recover with a compatible v0.3.0+ hotfix preserving stores and logical-v2 reader, or a separately verified encrypted backup in a clean profile.
- Do not begin ADR-002 encrypted-vault migration until backups and physical-device compatibility are verified.
